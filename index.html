FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Why does this cost more than a cheap freelancer?+
A cheap freelancer is selling you a $40 template. I am selling you planning, writing, custom design, custom built code, and 30 days of fine tuning. Different thing, different price.
How long does it take?+
One page site: 10 to 14 days. Full sales funnel: 2 to 3 weeks. Full site with brand: 4 to 6 weeks. The clock starts when you approve the writing, not when you pay.
How many changes can I ask for?+
Two rounds of design changes and unlimited writing changes before launch. The 30 days of fine tuning after launch catches anything else.
I already have a site. Can you just fix it?+
Sometimes. If the base is solid, I will review it and rebuild just the parts that bring in customers. If the base is weak, starting over is cheaper and faster than fixing it.
Do you just design, or do you build it too?+
Both. Every option comes as a finished, ready to launch website. You are not buying a picture, you are buying a real working site.
What happens after launch?+
30 days of free fine tuning based on real visitor data. After that, you can pay monthly for ongoing changes, new pages, and tests. No long term contract.

FAQ

Questions buyers actually ask.

Why does this cost more than the freelancer on Fiverr?+
Because that freelancer is selling you a template they paid $40 for. I am selling you strategy, copy, design, custom code, and 30 days of tuning. Different product, different price.
How long does it actually take?+
Landing page: 10 to 14 days. Conversion funnel: 2 to 3 weeks. Full site system: 4 to 6 weeks. Timelines start when copy is approved, not when the deposit clears.
How many revisions do I get?+
Two rounds of design revisions and unlimited copy revisions before launch. The 30 day post launch tuning window catches anything we missed.
I already have a site. Can you just fix it?+
Sometimes. If the bones are good, I will audit and rebuild the conversion sections. If the bones are bad, it is cheaper and faster to start fresh than to renovate.
Do you do design only, or code too?+
Both, by default. Every package ships as finished, hand coded HTML, CSS, and JavaScript ready to deploy. You are not buying a Figma file.
What happens after launch?+
30 days of free tuning against real traffic. After that, optional monthly retainer for ongoing copy tests, new pages, and conversion experiments. No lock in.
